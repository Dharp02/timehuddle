version: 1.0.4
date: 2026-09-29
title: Huddle's feed is now an inbox
---

The Huddle feed has a new shape: instead of one long scroll of cards, or a single
chat thread for the whole team, posts and clock sessions are grouped into
conversations you can switch between — the way an inbox works.

## Group by

A new **Group by** dropdown beside search groups your team's posts:

- **Session** — one conversation per clock-in-to-clock-out, with your plan and
  wrap-up alongside the clock times.
- **Day** — everyone's posts from one calendar day.
- **Person** — one conversation per teammate.
- **Ticket** — one conversation per ticket, plus a "No ticket" conversation for
  everything else.

Switching Group by is instant — it doesn't reload the feed, just re-groups what
is already there.

## Scope: your team, or everywhere you post

A row of team tabs lets you pick a team. Pick **Personal** to see your own posts
from every team you're in, in one place, still grouped by the same Group by
options.

## Posting

Each conversation you can post into has its own message box right where you're
reading it — a session or person conversation only if it's yours, a day or
ticket conversation for anyone. In **Personal**, a conversation that mixes posts
from several teams is read-only, so a reply never lands with the wrong team.
Editing your own post works the same way it always has.

The full composer (Pulse video, ticket picker) now only appears above the feed
for a team's very first post. **Drafts** still has it any time.

## What's different

- **Comments are not shown right now.** Nothing is deleted — your comment
  history is safe — but the inbox doesn't have a place to show replies yet.
- **Likes are not shown right now**, for the same reason: nothing to click on in
  the new message box yet. Your past likes aren't gone, they're just not visible.
- **Pulse video and the ticket picker aren't in a conversation's inline message
  box yet.** Use **Drafts** if you need either of those.

Deep links to a specific post — from a notification, or Dashboard → Recent
Activity — still take you straight to it, now opening that post's session
conversation instead of scrolling to a card.
